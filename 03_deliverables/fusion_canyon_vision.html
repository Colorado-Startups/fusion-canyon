The name

Named for the canyon. Built on the plain.

The corridor is not in a canyon, and it is better to say so first. Fort Collins sits at 5,003 ft on open plains. The canyon is 20 miles upstream and 5,000 ft up. It is the name because the Poudre comes out of it, the city drinks from it, and the irrigated ground the whole band sits on exists because of it.

Medicon Valley names a strait. Silicon Slopes names ground its companies are not on. A region takes the name of the landmark it faces.

The thesis

Same box. A quarter full. That is the whole project.

The container is already the right size. Silicon Valley's core covers 84 sq mi and the Fusion Canyon core covers 75, a difference of 11 percent. What differs is how full it is: 18,419 people per sq mi against 4,262, a factor of 4.3. Fill the ground Fusion Canyon already occupies at Silicon Valley's density and it holds 1.37 million. Density is the deliverable, and the room for it is 1,055,520 people.

Two things keep this honest. Silicon Valley's hull wraps part of San Francisco Bay, which is open water, so its density over land is higher than 18,419 and the target above is a floor rather than a ceiling. And the nearer rung matters more than the far one: Photon Mountain's density in this footprint is 436K, which is 1.4 times today rather than 4.3.
