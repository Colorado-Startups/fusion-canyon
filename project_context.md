## The idea

Fusion Canyon is the Colorado Startups region for Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor and Greeley, across Larimer and Weld counties. Its subject is laser-driven fusion: the lasers that drive it, and the supply chain those lasers need. The working tagline is "Build with fusion." It is a proposal, not a locked line.

## The name

The region is named for the canyon and built on the plain. Copy never places the region inside a canyon. The Cache la Poudre comes out of Poudre Canyon, Fort Collins drinks from it, and the irrigated ground the corridor sits on depends on it. That standing rule comes from the region data's `note` field.

## The core

- **Core cities:** Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor and Greeley.
- **Nearby:** Timnath, Wellington, Berthoud, Johnstown, Evans and Estes Park.
- **Proposal status:** the vision page calls the city set "a proposal, not a locked roster".

## Honesty rules

- No laser fusion machine anywhere puts power on a grid today. The page states this as an absence, and it anchors the whole project.
- "Substantial completion" never becomes "operating".
- A company's own figures are labeled as company-reported.
- No unattributed superlatives: never "leads", "first" or "world class" without a source.
- No dollar figure for the Northern Colorado fusion economy, because none exists.
- Derived numbers, such as 864,000 shots per day at 10 Hz, are labeled as arithmetic.

## Relation to the other regions

Photon Mountain (Boulder to Denver, computing with light) is the sister corridor, 50 miles south on I-25. The vision page sets the two up as a pair: one builds with light, and the other makes the power it runs on. Fusion Canyon reuses Photon Mountain's comparison-map method unchanged.
